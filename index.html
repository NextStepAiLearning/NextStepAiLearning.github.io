CURRICULUM

What participants learn

Our training is organised into practical modules. Each one ends with something participants can apply at work immediately.

Module 1AI Foundations for Work

How modern AI assistants think, what they do well, where they fail, and how to decide which tool suits which task.

Outcome: confident, informed tool choice
Module 2Prompting That Delivers

Giving AI full context, a clear role and a precise goal, then refining the result until it is ready to use.

Outcome: consistently useful answers
Module 3Writing & Communication

Professional emails, buyer replies, reports and translations written faster, in the right tone, and checked before sending.

Outcome: clear, polished communication
Module 4Excel, Data & Reports

Turning handwritten sheets and messy data into clean spreadsheets, formulas, summaries and review-ready reports.

Outcome: hours of manual work saved
Module 5Presentations & Meetings

Building slide decks with AI, and converting recordings and transcripts into minutes with owners and deadlines.

Outcome: faster decks, better follow-up
Module 6Automation & Responsible AI

Scheduled tasks and repeatable workflows, together with data safety, verification habits and knowing what AI should not do alone.

Outcome: safe, sustainable AI use
