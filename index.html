RESPONSIBLE AI

Using AI safely and professionally

AI is a powerful assistant, not an authority. These habits protect your company, your colleagues and your own reputation.

DoGood habits
  • Give clear context, then check every fact, figure and name in the answer.
  • Keep a human responsible for anything sent to a buyer, client or authority.
  • Use only tools your company allows for work data.
  • Remove personal and confidential details before you paste text.
  • Tell colleagues when AI helped, if your workplace expects it.
  • Compare spreadsheet totals with the original paper or system.
AvoidRisky habits
  • Pasting passwords, bank details, national ID numbers or private health data.
  • Sharing unreleased buyer prices, contracts or confidential designs with public tools.
  • Asking AI to invent experience, qualifications or references.
  • Trusting a confident answer without a source.
  • Recording meetings without telling participants.
  • Copying another brand’s content or a real person’s likeness.
