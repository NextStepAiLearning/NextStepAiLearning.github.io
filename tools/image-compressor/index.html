Image Compressor

Reduce the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP photos to an exact limit such as 50 KB or 100 KB, or by quality. Compress many images at once. Your photos never leave your device.

How to use the image compressor

  1. Drop your images into the box, or tap it to choose files. You can also paste an image.
  2. Choose "Target file size" and type a limit such as 100 KB, or choose "Quality" and move the slider.
  3. Pick the output format. WebP gives the smallest files; JPG works everywhere; PNG stays lossless.
  4. Press "Compress images", then download each file or all of them as one ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a photo to 100 KB or 50 KB?

Choose "Target file size", type the limit and press Compress. The tool lowers the quality step by step and, if needed, the dimensions, until the file fits under your limit.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The compression runs inside your browser. The files never leave your phone or computer.

Why does my PNG not get much smaller?

PNG is a lossless format, so quality cannot be reduced. Choose WebP or JPG for a much smaller file, or allow the tool to reduce the dimensions.

What is the best format for small files?

WebP is usually the smallest at the same quality. JPG is the safest for forms and email. Use PNG only for logos or images with transparency.

Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?

There is no account and no daily limit. Very large images (over about 60 megapixels) may fail on phones because browsers limit memory.
