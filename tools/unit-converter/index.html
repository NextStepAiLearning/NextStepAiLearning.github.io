Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature, area, volume and speed. Area includes the land units used in Bangladesh: decimal (shotok), katha and bigha.

How to use the unit converter

  1. Pick the type of unit (length, weight, area and so on).
  2. Type the value and choose the unit you have and the unit you want.
  3. Press Swap units to reverse the conversion, or Copy result to paste it.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in one katha?

This tool uses 720 square feet per katha, the common standard in Dhaka. Local values can differ, so check your land deed.

How many decimals (shotok) make one bigha?

With the values used here, 1 bigha = 20 katha = 14,400 ft², which is about 33.06 decimals. Regional bigha sizes vary.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. Choose Temperature, then Celsius to Fahrenheit, and the tool does it for you.

Is it accurate?

The factors are exact or standard international values, and the result is shown to about 10 significant digits.
