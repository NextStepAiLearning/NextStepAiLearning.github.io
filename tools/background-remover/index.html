Background Remover

Remove a plain background from a photo and get a transparent PNG, or place the subject on white, blue or red. Works by colour, so it suits studio-style photos, product shots and ID photos. Your picture never leaves your device.

Tap the picture on the background colour you want to remove (the corners are used automatically at first). Raise Tolerance if some background is left; lower it if the subject starts to disappear.

Honest limit. This tool removes a plain background by colour. It does not recognise people or objects, so hair against a busy background, shadows and subjects that share the background colour will not be cut out cleanly. Photos are processed at up to 2000 px on the longest side.

How to use the background remover

  1. Drop a photo that has a plain background, such as a wall, a sheet or a white table.
  2. Tap the background in the preview to choose its colour. The corners are used automatically at first.
  3. Raise Tolerance if background remains; lower it if the subject starts to disappear. Adjust edge shrink and softness for clean edges.
  4. Choose a transparent result or a solid colour, then download.

Frequently asked questions

Can this remove the background from any photo?

No. It removes a plain background by colour. Busy backgrounds, hair over a patterned wall, shadows, and subjects that have the same colour as the background will not be cut out cleanly. For those you need an AI tool or a photo editor.

How do I make a passport photo background white or blue?

Take the photo in front of a plain wall, remove the background here, choose White or Blue as the result background, then use the Passport Photo Maker to size it.

Why are there light edges around my subject?

Increase Edge shrink by one or two pixels, or raise Tolerance a little. Edge softness blends the border so it does not look cut with scissors.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

What is the maximum size?

Photos are processed at up to 2000 pixels on the longest side to keep phones responsive.
