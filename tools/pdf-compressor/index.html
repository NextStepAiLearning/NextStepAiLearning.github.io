PDF Compressor

Make scanned and photo PDFs much smaller for email, job portals and forms. The tool recompresses the pictures inside the PDF and keeps text sharp. Your PDF never leaves your device.

The tool shrinks the pictures inside the PDF, which is where scans and photo PDFs hold their size. Text stays sharp and selectable. Your files never leave your device.

How to use the pdf compressor

  1. Drop your PDF into the box, or tap it to choose files.
  2. Choose Recommended, or Strong for a smaller file. To meet a limit such as 500 KB, choose "Reach a target size".
  3. Press "Compress PDF". Large scans may take a little while on a phone.
  4. Download the compressed PDF and check it before you send it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a PDF to 200 KB or 500 KB?

Choose "Reach a target size", type the limit and press Compress. The tool tries stronger settings step by step until the file fits, or tells you honestly that it cannot go smaller.

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

PDFs made from text and drawings are already small, because they contain no photos. This tool shrinks the JPEG pictures inside a PDF, so scans and photo PDFs shrink a lot, while text-only PDFs stay almost the same.

Will the text become blurry?

No. Text and vector drawings are not changed. Only the pictures inside the PDF are recompressed, and you control how strongly.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The compression runs inside your browser, so confidential documents stay on your device.

Can it open a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password first, then compress the file. Damaged PDFs are refused with a clear message instead of being changed.

Which PDFs does it not shrink?

PDFs whose pictures are not JPEG, such as black-and-white fax-style scans, are kept as they are. If the tool cannot make a file smaller it keeps your original.
