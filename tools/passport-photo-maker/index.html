Passport Photo Maker

Turn a clear photo into a passport, visa or ID photo: choose the size, position the face, pick the background colour, keep the file under a size limit, and print many copies on one sheet. Your photo never leaves your device.

Drag the photo to move it, use the slider to zoom (zoom out below 100% to show the background colour around the photo). Keep the head centred, with a little space above the hair. The guide line marks the middle.

Check the rules. Each authority has its own rules (size, background, head height, no glasses, expression). This tool prepares the size and background; it does not guarantee acceptance. Always compare with the official requirement.

How to use the passport photo maker

  1. Choose a clear, front-facing photo with even light.
  2. Pick the photo size (for example 40×50 mm) and the background colour.
  3. Drag the photo to centre the face and use the zoom slider. The dashed guides are only for you and are not saved.
  4. Download the single photo, or the print sheet with many copies for 4×6 inch or A4 paper.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a Bangladesh passport photo?

Many Bangladesh passport and NID forms ask for 40×50 mm, and some older forms use 45×55 mm. Always check the size printed in your official instructions, because rules can change.

Does this tool remove the background?

No. It does not cut out a person. It places your photo on the background colour you choose, so use a photo taken in front of a plain, evenly lit wall. If the wall is not plain, retake the photo.

How do I keep the photo under 100 KB?

Type 100 in the maximum file size box before downloading. The tool lowers the JPG quality until the file fits.

How do I print passport photos at home?

Download the print sheet for 4×6 inch or A4, print at 100% (actual size, not fit to page) on photo paper, and cut along the thin lines.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
