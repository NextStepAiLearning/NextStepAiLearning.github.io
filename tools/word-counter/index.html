Frequently asked questions

Does it count Bangla words?

Yes. Words are counted by the spaces between them, so Bangla and English text both work. Sentences also end at the Bangla danda (।).

How is reading time calculated?

It assumes about 200 words per minute, a common average for adult readers.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your text is never sent to a server.

What does "Clean extra spaces" do?

It collapses repeated spaces, trims spaces around line breaks and removes more than one blank line in a row.
