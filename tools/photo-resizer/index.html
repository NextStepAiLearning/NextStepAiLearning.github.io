Photo Resizer

Resize photos by dimension, by file size, or both. Use pixels, centimetres, millimetres or inches, crop to an exact size, and set a maximum size in KB or MB. Your photos never leave your device.

How to use the photo resizer

  1. Drop your images in the box, or tap it to choose files.
  2. Choose the unit, then type a width, a height, or both. Pick a quick size such as Instagram or YouTube if you like.
  3. Choose what happens when the shape differs: keep proportions, crop to fill, add bars or stretch.
  4. Optionally set a maximum file size, then press Resize and download the result or a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize a photo to a specific width and height?

Type the width and height, choose "Fill the exact size (crop the edges)" and press Resize. The picture is scaled and centred so the result has exactly that size.

How do I resize in centimetres or inches?

Choose cm, mm or inches as the unit and set the resolution. At 300 DPI, 3.5 × 4.5 cm is 413 × 531 pixels.

How do I make a photo smaller than 50 KB and also a fixed size?

Set the width and height, then type 50 in the maximum file size box. The tool keeps your dimensions and lowers the JPG or WebP quality until the file fits.

Will enlarging a small photo make it sharper?

No. Enlarging only adds pixels, not detail, so it is off unless you tick the option.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
