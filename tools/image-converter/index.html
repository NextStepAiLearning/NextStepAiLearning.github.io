Image Format Converter

Convert pictures between JPG, PNG and WebP. Transparent areas are filled with a colour when you save as JPG. Convert many files at once and download a ZIP. Your files never leave your device.

How to use the image format converter

  1. Drop your pictures in the box, or tap it to choose files.
  2. Choose the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. For JPG or WebP set the quality. For JPG choose the colour that fills transparent areas.
  4. Press Convert, then download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert WebP to JPG?

Drop the WebP files, choose JPG and press Convert. Many websites save pictures as WebP, and forms or older apps often only accept JPG.

What happens to transparent PNG images when I save as JPG?

JPG cannot be transparent, so the transparent areas are filled with white, black or light grey, as you choose.

Does converting reduce quality?

PNG output is lossless. JPG and WebP are lossy; at 90% or more the difference is hard to see. Converting a JPG again and again slowly loses quality, so keep your original.

Can I convert HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Not every browser can open HEIC. If the file does not open, change the iPhone camera format to Most Compatible or export the photo as JPG first.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
