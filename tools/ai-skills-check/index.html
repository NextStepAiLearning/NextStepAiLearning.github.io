Frequently asked questions

Is the AI Skills Check really free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and does not store your answers.

Do I get a certificate?

No. This is a self-check without a certificate. Certified assessments are taken online by registered participants, and every certificate has a unique number and QR code for verification.

How is my score calculated?

Each correct answer gives one point, up to 10. The levels are guidance for your own learning, not an official grade.

Can I take it again?

Yes. The questions and the answer order change each time you start.

Who is it for?

Anyone who uses or plans to use AI tools at work, including students, office staff, managers and factory teams.
