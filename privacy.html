Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information NextStep AI Learning (“we”, “us”) collects through this website and the online assessment portal, why we collect it, and the choices you have. NextStep AI Learning is run by Al Akib, the trainer and director.

1. Information we collect

  • Application forms (training, certificate and assessment requests): your name, mobile number, email address, organisation, job title, training details, location, preferred timing and any notes you write.
  • Online assessment: your registered mobile number and name, your answers and score, the date, a unique certificate number and, if the administrator added one, a photo for your certificate.
  • Technical data: we do not run advertising trackers or analytics. Our hosting and service providers (see section 4) may keep ordinary technical logs such as IP address and browser type.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your request and contact you about training, assessments or certificates.
  • To run the assessment, calculate your result and issue your certificate.
  • To let anyone who holds your certificate number or QR code verify that the certificate is genuine.
  • To send you emails about your request, such as a confirmation and status updates.

3. Certificate verification is public

When someone enters a certificate number or scans its QR code, the verification page shows the certificate holder’s name, training title, organisation, score and issue date. It does not show your phone number or email address.

4. Where your data is stored and who processes it

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form and assessment data is stored in a private Google Sheet and processed with Google Apps Script. Emails are sent through Google. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep application records as long as needed to handle your request and follow up. We keep a minimal certificate record for as long as the certificate should remain verifiable.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing alakib.rzn@protonmail.com. If you ask us to delete a certificate record, that certificate can no longer be verified online.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS and access to the data is limited to the administrator. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Who this is for

Our training and assessments are intended for adults and working professionals.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact
